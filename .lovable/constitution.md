# HUMAN OPERATING SYSTEM ECOSYSTEM (HOSE)
## Technical Constitution — Version 1.0

> Canonical governance spec. Every present and future component of the ecosystem must comply.
> When this document and `.lovable/plan.md` disagree, the Constitution wins and the plan is updated.

---

## PREAMBLE

The Human Operating System Ecosystem (HOSE) is a personal technology platform designed around one central principle:

**The human being is the primary operating system. Every other system exists to enhance, protect, and optimize the human.**

The ecosystem consists of:
- A central Human Kernel
- Specialized Operating Systems
- Intelligence infrastructure
- Shared communication standards
- Personal data governance
- AI-driven decision support

---

## ARTICLE I — THE CORE PRINCIPLE

**The Human Comes First.**

The ecosystem does NOT optimize: revenue alone, productivity alone, wealth alone, efficiency alone.

It optimizes **Sustainable Human Performance** — balancing achievement, health, relationships, learning, recovery, financial security, personal growth, long-term fulfillment.

A recommendation that improves one area while destroying the human operating state is a failed optimization.

---

## ARTICLE II — ECOSYSTEM STRUCTURE

```
              HUMAN KERNEL
                   |
          PLATFORM FOUNDATION
                   |
        SPECIALIZED OPERATING SYSTEMS
```

**Layer 1 — Human Kernel:** identity, human state, personal context, capacity, values, standards, preferences, decision principles. Does NOT manage specialized domains.

**Layer 2 — Platform Foundation:** engine registration, permissions, events, APIs, data standards, synchronization, version compatibility.

**Layer 3 — Specialized Operating Systems:** each owns one domain (Trading, Business, Finance, Asset, Health, …). Independent but connected.

---

## ARTICLE III — THE KERNEL BOUNDARY

**Kernel OWNS:**
- Identity: mission, values, principles, strengths, weaknesses, standards, preferences
- Human State: energy, recovery, focus, mental/emotional state, physical readiness, stress, capacity
- Personal Intelligence: recommendations, decisions, reflections, learning, personal policies
- Daily Operating: schedule, priorities, routines, personal optimization

**Kernel does NOT own:** business transactions, trading records, financial transactions, project data, asset ownership records. Those belong to specialized OS systems.

---

## ARTICLE IV — SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH

Every piece of data has exactly one owner.

- Trading profit → owned by Trading OS; consumed by Finance OS, Kernel, Achievements.
- Business revenue → owned by Business OS; consumed by Finance OS, Kernel.
- Health metrics → owned by Health OS; consumed by Kernel, performance systems.

No duplicated ownership.

---

## ARTICLE V — OPERATING SYSTEM CONTRACT

Every OS must implement the Engine Contract and declare:

1. **Identity** — name, version, purpose, owner, capabilities.
2. **Ownership** — what data it owns.
3. **Published Signals** — what it emits (e.g. Trading OS: TradingPerformanceScore, RiskExposure, TradingStress, ProfitSignal, DisciplineScore).
4. **Required Signals** — what it consumes (e.g. Trading OS: EnergyState, FocusState, RecoveryState, FinancialContext).

---

## ARTICLE VI — PLATFORM FOUNDATION

**Engine Registry** — every installed OS: name, version, status, dependencies, capabilities, permissions, APIs, events.

**Lifecycle Management** — install, activate, deactivate, update, migrate versions, remove, compatibility checks.

**Permission Manager** — every OS declares data requested, data provided, data prohibited. Users control access.

---

## ARTICLE VII — EVENT ARCHITECTURE

Systems communicate only through events. No direct system-to-system dependency.

```
    Operating System
          |
      Event Bus
          |
    Other Systems
```

Examples:
- `WorkoutCompleted` → Health OS, Achievements, Kernel.
- `TradeCompleted` → Finance OS, Kernel, Achievements.

---

## ARTICLE VIII — DATA STANDARDIZATION

Every signal MUST contain: **type, value, source, timestamp, confidence, reliability, trend, metadata**.

Example `EnergyState`: `{ value: 82, source: "Health OS", confidence: 0.92, trend: "improving", timestamp: <Date> }`.

---

## ARTICLE IX — INTELLIGENCE ARCHITECTURE

- **Human State Engine** — understands current condition.
- **Intelligence Orchestrator** — combines info from all systems: context creation, conflict resolution, prioritization, recommendations.
- **Recommendation Engine** — every recommendation MUST include: reason, evidence, confidence, alternative, expected outcome.

---

## ARTICLE X — AI GOVERNANCE

The AI MUST: explain (no unexplained decisions), admit uncertainty, learn from feedback, respect permissions, optimize sustainably (never sacrifice long-term human performance for short-term gains).

---

## ARTICLE XI — PERSONAL OPERATING POLICIES

Users may define permanent rules that act as constraints on AI recommendations.

Examples:
- "If sleep is below six hours, reduce workload."
- "Never make financial decisions while emotionally unstable."
- "Recovery follows high-intensity periods."

---

## ARTICLE XII — LEARNING LOOP

`Observe → Recommend → Execute → Measure → Reflect → Learn → Improve`. The system personalizes over time.

---

## ARTICLE XIII — FUTURE INTELLIGENCE ROADMAP

- **Phase 1 — Platform Foundation:** Event Bus, Engine Registry, Permissions, Schemas.
- **Phase 2 — Human Intelligence:** Human State Models, Recommendations, Feedback loops.
- **Phase 3 — Personal Intelligence:** Pattern recognition, Experiments, Policies.
- **Phase 4 — Advanced Intelligence:** Digital Twin, Knowledge Graph, Simulations.
- **Phase 5 — Open Ecosystem:** Marketplace, Third-party Engines, Developer Platform.

---

## ARTICLE XIV — SECURITY AND PRIVACY

Human data is the highest-value asset. Minimum necessary access, explicit permissions, encryption, audit trails, data portability, user ownership. The user owns the data; the platform serves the user.

---

## ARTICLE XV — FINAL CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLE

Every future design decision must answer:

> "Does this improve the human operating condition while strengthening the ecosystem?"

If yes: build it. If no: reject it.

The purpose is not more software. The purpose is a technology environment where a human consistently becomes healthier, smarter, more disciplined, more capable, more fulfilled, more aligned with their highest potential.
